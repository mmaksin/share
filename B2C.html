6 · Health passport
One account, multiple principals — the gatekeeper persona switches between them. Active principal is always visible to avoid mis-attribution.

Why these decisions, in brief

Three, never zero

If no perfect matches exist, we still show three — explicitly labelled imperfect, with the reasons spelled out. Echoes the B2B PRD §3.3.

Discretion as a feature

No likes, no public profiles, no "other guests viewed this." Sharing is a private, expiring link or a printable PDF.

Concierge always one tap

The gold pill on the home screen never goes away. Default channel is WhatsApp — that's where this segment already lives.

Multi-principal by design

Spouses, parents, and family-office gatekeepers manage multiple profiles from one device. Active profile is always visible.

Halal & gender as filters, not afterthoughts

Halal kitchen, gender-segregated spa, female-only practitioners — first-class metadata, surfaced on every venue card.

Explain, never assert

The "Why this venue" panel cites which goals it addresses, which methods map to which conditions, and what makes the match imperfect.
